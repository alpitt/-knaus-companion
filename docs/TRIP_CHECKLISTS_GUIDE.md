# Trip checklists guide

Checklist items retain phase, status, source, required/blocking flags, evidence and measurement references. Generic templates are copied into a trip; the template itself is never modified.

Required skipped items and all waivers need a recorded reason. Failed or incomplete blocking departure items prevent a ready-to-depart assessment. Reset is explicit, and completion does not prove roadworthiness.

The initial templates contain only generic weekend, arrival and return reminders. Owners should review every item for their vehicle and journey and may leave unknown information visible.
