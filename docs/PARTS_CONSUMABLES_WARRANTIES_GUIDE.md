# Parts, consumables and warranties guide

Part records may contain owner-entered manufacturer, model, part/serial/batch identifiers, cost, evidence, installation/removal details and component links. Replacement explicitly links old and new records; it never edits the Digital Twin reference component.

Consumable records preserve original quantities and units. Every use, refill, replacement and expiry value is owner-entered. No capacity or consumption is inferred.

Warranty records use entered providers, dates, terms, evidence and related component/part/workshop IDs. Unknown dates remain unknown; Companion does not create claims or fabricate terms. All metadata stays local, appears in ordinary backups and can be included in printable reports.
