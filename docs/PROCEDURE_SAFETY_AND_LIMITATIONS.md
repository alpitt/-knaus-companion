# Procedure Safety and Limitations

Confidence describes recorded source support, not safety. Owner-authored and generic material is not manufacturer documentation. Owner review is not professional approval. Missing hazards, limits or acceptance criteria remain unknown and visible. Professional review is recorded only when explicitly supplied.

Completion does not prove work quality, safety, roadworthiness, legal compliance or certification. Evidence and measurements are retained without binary interpretation or automatic pass/fail unless an explicit sourced criterion exists. Emergency material cannot replace emergency services or competent professional judgement.

## Prohibited practices

Never fabricate a procedure, step, manufacturer instruction, torque, voltage, pressure, tolerance, acceptance criterion, hazard or professional review. Never claim owner review equals professional approval, completion proves safety/roadworthiness, or evidence proves quality. Never automatically approve a procedure, verify an execution, resolve a fault, complete maintenance/Change Plans, close Workshop Jobs, pass inspections, update installed component state, overwrite Digital Twin/System Graph reference data, interpret evidence binaries, upload procedure data, erase revision history or silently overwrite imports.
