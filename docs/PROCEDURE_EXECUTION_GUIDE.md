# Procedure Execution Guide

## Readiness and start

Readiness reports only configured prerequisites: source, tools, parts, consumables, PPE, evidence, measurements, inspection, workshop or professional review. Availability is never inferred. **Ready to start** does not mean safe. Starting requires explicit confirmation.

## Guided mode

An execution pins the exact procedure version. Complete, skip, defer, block or fail each step explicitly. Skipping a mandatory step creates a visible deviation. Branches and hold points are deterministic; professional-review holds cannot be silently bypassed. Notes, evidence IDs and measurement IDs are references only and are not interpreted.

## Recovery

Pause and resume persist locally. Checkpoints contain a local integrity hash, not certification. Restoring or deleting a checkpoint requires confirmation and never erases newer history.

## Completion

Completion review lists incomplete steps, deviations, missing evidence/measurements and follow-up actions. Partial completion and failure remain historical. Completion and verification are separate explicit actions. Owner Record, maintenance, Workshop Job, inspection and revision updates are previews; linked records do not change automatically.

Reports are local records, not manufacturer instructions, professional certification, roadworthiness or legal compliance.
