# Diagnostic safety and limitations

An investigation is not a diagnosis. A supported conclusion is not a confirmed root cause. Owner confirmation is not professional review; recorded professional metadata does not verify qualifications. Source confidence, measurement method, calibration, evidence, tested scope, and unknowns limit every result. No-fault-found applies only to tested scope. Escalation acknowledgement does not clear risk. The application does not certify roadworthiness, legal compliance, emergency response, or safe system use.

Never fabricate a diagnostic, branch, symptom, measurement, unit, limit, tolerance, acceptance criterion, hazard, emergency instruction, or manufacturer guidance. Never infer root cause automatically, present a supported conclusion as confirmed cause, claim no fault outside tested scope, rank causes probabilistically, recommend a repair automatically, resolve a fault automatically, complete maintenance, close a Workshop Job, pass an inspection, complete a Change Plan, change Vehicle Health, update component state, overwrite Digital Twin or System Graph data, interpret evidence binaries, upload diagnostic data, erase revision history, or silently overwrite imports.
