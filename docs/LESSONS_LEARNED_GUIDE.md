# Lessons Learned Guide

Record a lesson against the exact procedure version, execution and step. Link evidence or measurements by ID; they do not prove general applicability. Categories include unclear/missing steps, tools, parts, evidence gaps, unexpected conditions, source conflicts, safety observations and owner tips.

A lesson can be drafted, reviewed, rejected, accepted for revision, incorporated, superseded or archived. Rejected lessons remain historical. Creating or accepting a revision proposal never changes a procedure; incorporation requires an explicitly confirmed new, unapproved version. No technical conclusion or confidence increase is automatic.
