# Ownership Analytics guide

Ownership analytics describe only the records supplied. Every statistic should be read with its sample size, date coverage, currency group and missing inputs.

- Workshop analytics count recorded activity; they do not rate a Workshop.
- Maintenance analytics do not claim vehicle condition.
- Fault analytics do not create a reliability score or root-cause diagnosis.
- Trip analytics use recorded distance and nights and never infer location.
- Recurring-pattern analysis uses deterministic explicit identifiers and labels results as patterns, not diagnoses.
- Comparisons are descriptive; missing data is not zero and “improved” requires a visible metric.

Correlation does not prove causation. Low-confidence or incomplete sources remain visible and reduce the usefulness of the result.
