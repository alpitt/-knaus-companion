# Ownership Costs guide

Add costs only when you intend to create an explicit ledger record. Amount, currency and amount type are preserved exactly; source-module amounts are preview candidates and never become ledger entries automatically.

## Actuals, estimates, refunds and reimbursements

Choose the appropriate amount type. Estimates, quotes and budgets remain separate from actuals. Refunds and reimbursements are reported separately and do not rewrite the original invoice.

## Allocations and reconciliation

Allocations may target components, systems, trips, maintenance, Workshop Jobs, modifications, parts, consumables or an owner-defined category. Partial allocation remains visible. An allocation cannot exceed the source amount, percentages cannot exceed 100%, and one cost cannot allocate into a different currency. Duplicate candidates are shown for review and are never merged automatically.

## Multiple currencies and TCO

Each currency has an independent total. No exchange rate or conversion is applied. TCO exposes actual, estimated, refunded, reimbursed and excluded records. Cost per distance, night or trip is “not calculable” when its explicit denominator is missing.

Printing and exports are local. These figures are owner records, not audited accounts, tax advice or accounting advice. Invoice and receipt references are optional and should be treated as private.
